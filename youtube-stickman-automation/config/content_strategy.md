# Content Strategy — Five Stickman Blueprints

Fed into Stage 1 (Topic Discovery) alongside `channel_blueprint.yaml`, so
every generated batch of ideas follows this playbook instead of generic
listicle topics. Update this file as you learn what actually performs —
it's a living document, not a one-time seed.

## Positioning

**Five Stickman Blueprint** — *Complex ideas. Simple visuals. Actionable frameworks.*

Every idea must answer three questions:
1. What is the complex idea?
2. Can I understand it visually in 30 seconds?
3. What can I actually do with it today?

## The winning formula

**Counterintuitive hook → simple framework → 3-5 concrete steps → punchy
takeaway.**

Viewers stay for a framework they can actually use, not vague motivation.
Every video should:
1. Open with a claim that contradicts what people assume ("Why smart
   people stay poor," not "5 tips for saving money").
2. Name a simple framework, bias, law, or rule — something quotable and
   memorable (Pareto Principle, Parkinson's Law, the 1% Rule, the Habit
   Loop, the Wealth Flywheel).
3. Break it into 3-5 concrete, actionable steps — not abstract advice.
4. Land on a punchy, repeatable takeaway the viewer can restate to
   someone else.

## The CPM insight

**Money/psychology crossover topics pull the highest CPM** — they attract
finance advertisers on top of the broad psychology/self-improvement
audience. When scoring `monetization_potential` in Stage 1, weight topics
that combine a psychological bias/law with a money angle above pure
psychology or pure productivity topics. AI + wealth crossovers also rank
high.

## The five pillars

Ideas should map to one of these five pillars. Rotate coverage so no
single pillar dominates a week; aim for roughly balanced pillar mix
across every batch of ideas Stage 1 generates.

### 1. Mindset & Psychology
Promise: explain why people think, behave and decide the way they do.
Examples: psychology of procrastination, why you overthink, dopamine &
motivation, growth vs fixed mindset, psychology of discipline, why
smart people make bad decisions.
Visual formula: stickman → problem → brain/psychology concept →
framework → action.

### 2. Wealth & Money
Promise: make money principles simple enough to understand in 5-10 min.
Examples: how the wealthy think about money, assets vs liabilities,
compound-interest snowball, lifestyle inflation, good debt vs bad debt,
the wealth-building flywheel, why saving alone won't make you rich.
Visual formula: stickman → money problem → simple diagram →
wealth principle → 3 actions.

### 3. Productivity & Habits
Promise: turn productivity science into systems people can actually use.
Examples: 2-minute rule, deep work, habit loop, time blocking, the 1%
improvement system, environment design, minimum viable day, beating
phone addiction.
Visual formula: overwhelmed stickman → system introduced → before/after
→ actionable challenge.

### 4. Career Growth & Self-Improvement
Promise: practical frameworks for becoming more valuable at work.
Examples: how to become impossible to ignore, career capital flywheel,
promotions without 80-hour weeks, salary negotiation, personal brand,
the 5 levels of professional value, career reinvention.
Visual formula: stickman at Level 1 → obstacle → framework → Level
2/3/4/5 → action plan.

### 5. AI & The Future of Work
Promise: explain AI without jargon and show people how to use it.
Examples: AI explained for normal people, AI skills that matter, AI to
save 10 hours a week, AI agents explained, ChatGPT vs agents, AI for
learning, jobs AI will change first, prompting vs thinking, staying
valuable in an AI world.
Visual formula: stickman doing task manually → AI enters → workflow
diagram → result → practical prompt/tool.

## Long-form structure (used by Stage 2)

Keep almost every long-form video structurally consistent — this is the
skeleton Stage 2 should follow when it writes the script:

- **0:00 Hook** — "Imagine two stickmen earning the exact same salary…"
- **0:20 Problem** — show the common mistake
- **1:00 Big Idea** — introduce the psychology/economic/productivity concept
- **2:00 Blueprint** — turn it into a simple 3-5 step framework
- **5:00 Real Example** — follow one stickman applying it
- **7:00 Action Plan** — give viewers 3 things to do
- **8:00 Challenge** — "Try this for the next 7 days"
- **End** — "Follow for the next Stickman Blueprint"

## Weekly cadence (context for Shorts vs long-form choices)

- 2 long-form videos/week (Tue + Fri).
- 2 Shorts/day — Shorts feed the long-form videos: each week's Shorts
  should support the same theme as that week's long-form pieces rather
  than be 14 unrelated ideas.

## Starter topics (examples of the pattern — don't repeat verbatim)

Reference examples of the formula working, ranked by CPM + shareability.

runs, not reuse this list once it's exhausted — treat it as "here's what

10. "How to Use AI to 10x Your Income in 2026" — AI + wealth crossover

## Instruction for topic generation

When generating new ideas (with or without a seed idea from the user):
- Every idea maps to exactly one of the five pillars — tag it mentally.
- Apply the winning formula to every idea's framing.
- Aim for roughly balanced pillar coverage across each batch.
- Favor money/psychology and AI/wealth crossover angles when scoring
  monetization potential, per the CPM insight.
- Use the starter topics as a style/quality bar, not a source to copy —
  generate fresh titles, frameworks, and hooks each time.
