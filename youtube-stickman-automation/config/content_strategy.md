# Content Strategy — What Makes This Channel's Videos Blow Up

Fed into Stage 1 (Topic Discovery) alongside `channel_blueprint.yaml`, so
every generated batch of ideas follows this formula instead of generic
listicle topics. Update this file as you learn what actually performs —
it's a living playbook, not a one-time seed.

## The winning formula

**Counterintuitive hook → simple framework → 3-5 concrete steps → punchy
takeaway.**

Viewers stay for a framework they can actually use, not vague motivation.
Every video should:
1. Open with a claim that contradicts what people assume ("Why smart
   people stay poor," not "5 tips for saving money").
2. Name a simple framework or bias/law/rule — something quotable and
   memorable (Pareto Principle, Parkinson's Law, the 1% Rule).
3. Break it into 3-5 concrete, actionable steps — not abstract advice.
4. Land on a punchy, repeatable takeaway the viewer can restate to someone
   else.

## The CPM insight

**Money/psychology crossover topics pull the highest CPM** — they attract
finance advertisers on top of the broad psychology/self-improvement
audience. When scoring `monetization_potential` in Stage 1, weight
topics that combine a psychological bias/law with a money angle above
pure psychology or pure productivity topics.

## Starter topics (examples of the pattern — don't repeat verbatim)

These are reference examples of the formula working, ranked by CPM +
shareability. Stage 1 should generate **new** ideas in this same spirit
each time it runs, not reuse this list once it's exhausted — treat it as
"here's what good looks like," not a fixed queue.

1. "The 1% Rule That Made Warren Buffett Rich" — compounding explained simply
2. "Why Smart People Stay Poor (The Bird in Hand Bias)" — psychology of risk aversion — *money/psychology crossover*
3. "The 2-Minute Rule That Fixes Procrastination Forever" — productivity framework
4. "Parkinson's Law: Why You Never Have Enough Time" — psychology + productivity crossover
5. "How AI Will Replace These 5 Jobs by 2030" — AI/career fear-based hook, huge CTR
6. "The Pareto Principle: Do 20% of This, Get 80% Results" — evergreen framework video
7. "Loss Aversion: The Bias That's Keeping You Broke" — psychology + money — *money/psychology crossover*
8. "5 Habits of People Who Retire Before 40" — FIRE/wealth niche, high CPM advertisers — *money/psychology crossover*
9. "The Dopamine Trap: Why You Can't Focus Anymore" — psychology, huge search volume
10. "How to Use AI to 10x Your Income in 2026" — timely, AI + wealth combo

## Instruction for topic generation

When generating new ideas (with or without a seed idea from the user):
- Apply the winning formula above to every idea's framing.
- Favor money/psychology crossover angles when scoring monetization
  potential, per the CPM insight.
- Use the starter topics as a style/quality bar, not a source to copy —
  generate fresh titles, frameworks, and hooks each time.
